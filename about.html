Hello, I'm Vinicius Richter

Forest Data Scientist | Machine Learning Engineer | Software Developer

I am a Data Scientist with experience in applied statistics, predictive modeling, machine learning, deep learning, data analysis, and the development of data-driven solutions. I work on building models and systems to automate analytical processes, extract insights from large volumes of data, and support decision-making in real-world problems.

My background combines Forest Engineering, a Master's degree in Forest Engineering, and a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, providing a multidisciplinary view that integrates statistics, programming, artificial intelligence, and domain knowledge. I currently apply this combination mainly to the development of data science solutions, with the forestry sector as my main field of application.

Throughout my career, I have developed solutions involving statistical modeling, machine learning, neural networks, computer vision, geospatial data processing, and remote sensing. I also have experience in analytical workflow automation, dashboard development, data processing and integration, and the development of software tools focused on analysis and decision-making.

I am the author of the ForestPyTools library and the FPT PlotAllocation QGIS plugin, projects that demonstrate my experience turning analytical problems into reusable, scalable computational solutions.

I currently work as Forest Biometry Analyst – Level II (Forest Data Scientist) at Treevia Forest Technologies, where I develop and maintain statistics and data science–based solutions involving predictive modeling, LiDAR data processing, geoprocessing, analytical automation, and the evaluation of new methodologies.

My experience in the forestry sector is a domain differentiator, but my main professional focus is on Data Science, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Statistics, and the development of computational solutions capable of turning data into results.
